Lesson 1: Introduction to Tally Prime & Company Creation

Reading time: 15 minutes | Level: Beginner

What is Tally Prime?

Tally Prime is a comprehensive accounting software widely used by businesses to manage financial transactions. It automates accounting processes, reduces errors, and saves time.

Importance of Tally

  • Handles complex accounting tasks with ease
  • Maintains books of accounts and generates financial statements
  • Complies with GST (Goods and Services Tax) requirements
  • Manages inventory, payroll, and banking
  • Supports multiple languages and currencies
  • Highly demanded skill in the job market

Tally Prime Interface

  • Gateway of Tally: Main screen — access all features from here
  • Menu Bar: Company, Data, Exchange, Go To
  • Button Bar: Quick access buttons on the right side
  • Calculator: Built-in calculator (Ctrl + N)
  • Info Panel: Shows company details and period

Company Creation

Steps to create a company in Tally Prime:

  1. Open Tally Prime
  2. Go to: Gateway → Create Company (Alt + F3 → Create)
  3. Fill in details:
    • Company Name: Your business name
    • Address: Business address
    • Country: India
    • State: Your state
    • Financial Year From: Usually 1-Apr
    • Books Beginning From: Date when you start
  4. Accept the form (Ctrl + A or Enter)

Ledger and Group Setup

Ledger: An account head for recording transactions (e.g., Cash, Bank, Sales, Purchase)

Groups: Categories that organize ledgers (e.g., Current Assets, Current Liabilities)

Pre-defined Groups in Tally

  • Capital Account, Current Assets, Current Liabilities
  • Direct/Indirect Expenses, Direct/Indirect Incomes
  • Fixed Assets, Investments, Loans
  • Purchase Accounts, Sales Accounts
  • Bank Accounts, Cash-in-Hand

Creating a Ledger

  1. Gateway → Create → Ledger
  2. Enter Name (e.g., "HDFC Bank")
  3. Select Group (e.g., "Bank Accounts")
  4. Accept (Ctrl + A)

Key Points to Remember
  • Tally Prime is accounting software for managing finances
  • Gateway of Tally is the main screen
  • Create Company: Alt+F3 → Create
  • Ledger = account head; Groups = categories of ledgers
  • Ctrl+A = Accept/Save; Esc = Go Back
