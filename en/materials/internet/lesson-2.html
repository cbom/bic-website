Lesson 2: Email, Cyber Security & AI Basics

Reading time: 15 minutes | Level: Beginner

Email (Electronic Mail)

Email is a method of sending and receiving messages electronically. Popular email services: Gmail, Yahoo Mail, Outlook.

Creating a Gmail Account

  1. Go to gmail.com → Click "Create account"
  2. Enter your name, choose a username (e.g., yourname@gmail.com)
  3. Set a strong password
  4. Add phone number for recovery
  5. Accept terms and start using

Email Features

  • Compose: Write new email
  • To: Recipient's email address
  • Subject: Brief topic of the email
  • Attachments: Send files (documents, photos) with the email
  • CC (Carbon Copy): Send copy to additional people
  • BCC (Blind Carbon Copy): Send copy without others knowing
  • Reply/Forward: Respond to or share received emails

Video Conferencing

Tools for online meetings and classes:

  • Zoom: Popular for meetings, webinars, classes
  • Google Meet: Free with Google account, works in browser
  • Microsoft Teams: For office collaboration

Cyber Security

Cyber security protects computers and data from threats and unauthorized access.

Common Threats

  • Virus: Malicious program that damages files and spreads to other computers
  • Phishing: Fake emails/websites that trick you into giving personal information
  • Spam: Unwanted junk emails
  • Hacking: Unauthorized access to computer systems
  • Ransomware: Locks your files and demands money to unlock

Password Safety

  • Use strong passwords (8+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
  • Never share passwords with anyone
  • Use different passwords for different accounts
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) when available
  • Change passwords regularly

Safe Browsing Tips

  • Look for HTTPS (🔒) in the address bar before entering information
  • Don't click suspicious links in emails
  • Don't download files from unknown sources
  • Keep antivirus software updated
  • Use firewall protection

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence — like understanding speech, recognizing images, and making decisions.

AI in Daily Life

  • Voice Assistants: Google Assistant, Alexa, Siri
  • ChatGPT: AI that can answer questions and write text
  • Auto-correct: Phone keyboard suggestions
  • Recommendations: YouTube/Netflix suggesting content
  • Face Recognition: Phone unlock, security systems
Key Points to Remember
  • Email: Compose, To, Subject, Attachment, CC, BCC
  • Cyber threats: Virus, Phishing, Hacking, Ransomware
  • Strong passwords: 8+ chars, mixed, unique per account
  • Look for HTTPS, don't click suspicious links
  • AI is used in assistants, recommendations, face recognition
